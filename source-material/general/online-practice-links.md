## Third-Party Practice Resources

### Fully free — interactive online tests

1. **IELTS Online Tests** — https://ieltsonlinetests.com
   The biggest third-party platform, with 120+ free full mock tests (Listening, Reading, Writing, Speaking) in a computer interface that mimics the real exam. Includes instant scoring, answer explanations, and AI writing/speaking feedback. Widely considered the best free third-party option.

2. **Exam English** — https://www.examenglish.com/IELTS/
   Free interactive practice tests for Listening, Academic Reading, General Training Reading, Academic Writing, General Writing, Speaking, Vocabulary, and Grammar. No account required.
   - [Listening](https://www.examenglish.com/IELTS/IELTS_listening.html)
   - [Academic Reading](https://www.examenglish.com/IELTS/IELTS_Academic_reading.html)
   - [General Reading](https://www.examenglish.com/IELTS/IELTS_General_reading.html)
   - [Academic Writing](https://www.examenglish.com/IELTS/IELTS_Academic_writing.html)
   - [General Writing](https://www.examenglish.com/IELTS/IELTS_General_writing.html)
   - [Speaking](https://www.examenglish.com/IELTS/IELTS_Speaking.htm)
   - [Vocabulary](https://www.examenglish.com/IELTS/IELTSVocabulary.htm)
   - [Grammar](https://www.examenglish.com/IELTS/ielts_grammar_test.htm)

3. **Practice9** — https://practice9.co/free-ielts-tests
   Free computer-based IELTS practice tests designed to simulate the real exam interface closely. Well-regarded for realism.

4. **IELTS Mentor** — https://www.ielts-mentor.com
   Free sample questions, reading passages, listening tests, writing tasks, and mock tests with model answers. Also has downloadable answer sheets and IELTS handbooks.

5. **IELTS Buddy** — https://www.ieltsbuddy.com/ielts-practice-tests.html
   Free online practice tests for all four sections (Reading, Listening, Writing, Speaking), based on real IELTS test format.

6. **IELTSMaterial.com** — https://ieltsmaterial.com/ielts-practice-tests/
   Free section-specific practice tests with sample answers and tips. Also has recent actual test questions with model answers for all four skills.

### Free with registration / limited free tier

7. **BestMyTest** — https://www.bestmytest.com/ielts/practice-test
   1 full free practice test (all four sections, ~2h45m) without payment. Tests 2–16 and simulation tests require a paid subscription. Good quality interface.

8. **IDP IELTS Prepare** — https://ielts.idp.com/prepare
   IDP (official partner) offers free preparation articles, speaking and writing tips, and some sample test content. Their actual downloadable practice PDFs with answers are accessible from their regional pages (e.g. https://ieltsidpindia.com/prepare/ielts-academic-practice).

### Notable mentions (sample questions & tips, less interactive)

9. **Physics Wallah / pw.live** — https://www.pw.live/study-abroad/ielts/exams
    Free and paid mock tests available online, popular in South Asia.

10. **GeeksforGeeks IELTS** — https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/ielts/
    Full-length free IELTS practice tests online, recently expanded their test library.

> **Standout picks:** IELTS Online Tests (ieltsonlinetests.com) for the most comprehensive free interactive experience, and Exam English for quick no-signup section-specific drills. Both complement the official materials nicely.
